# Form 16 Module

[← Back to index](../README.md)

Form 16 is the TDS certificate an Indian employer must issue to every employee whose salary had tax
deducted. It is a legal document: the employee files their return from it, and the tax department
cross-checks it against what the employer actually deposited.

This is the most intricate module in the platform — roughly 900 lines of dedicated service code
(`services/form16.py`) implementing the statutory structure.

---

## 1. What the certificate contains

| | Part A | Part B |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Proof of tax deducted and deposited | Computation of how that tax was arrived at |
| **Contains** | Employer TAN/PAN, employee PAN, employment period, quarterly TDS summary, challan references | Gross salary, exemptions, deductions, taxable income, tax, surcharge, cess, relief |
| **Source** | TDS records + deposit/challan entries | Salary aggregation + approved declarations |

The platform generates both, plus a **combined PDF**.

---

## 2. Prerequisites

### Employer setup

![Employer setup](../screenshots/form16-employer.png)

TAN, PAN, name and address of the deductor — printed on every certificate and used by the tax
department to match deposits. Configured once per financial year.

### Financial year

Certificates are always scoped to a financial year (1 April – 31 March). The module derives the
correct month set and quarter boundaries from it rather than assuming a calendar year.

---

## 3. Employee declarations

![Declarations review](../screenshots/form16-declarations.png)

Before a certificate can be accurate, investment declarations must be settled.

**Flow:** employee declares → uploads proof → HR reviews → approves or rejects with reason →
approved amounts enter the computation.

**Only approved declarations reduce taxable income.** This is a deliberate control: an unverified
claim that silently lowers TDS creates a shortfall the employee discovers at assessment, with
interest. Requiring verification moves that discovery to a point where it can still be fixed.

### Deduction caps enforced

| Section | Cap | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 80C | ₹1,50,000 | LIC, PPF, ELSS, principal repayment, tuition fees |
| 80CCD(1B) | ₹50,000 | Additional NPS, over and above 80C |
| 80D — self | ₹25,000 | Health insurance premium |
| 80D — parents | ₹25,000 | Parents' health insurance |
| 80TTA | ₹10,000 | Savings account interest |
| 24(b) | ₹2,00,000 | Home loan interest on self-occupied property |
| House property loss | ₹2,00,000 | Set-off ceiling |

Caps are applied by dedicated functions (`cap_80c`, `cap_80ccd_1b`, `cap_80d`, `cap_80tta`) rather
than inline arithmetic, so an employee declaring ₹3,00,000 of 80C gets ₹1,50,000 of relief — never
the declared figure.

### HRA exemption

HRA exemption is the **least of three**:

1. Actual HRA received
2. Rent paid − 10% of basic salary
3. 50% of basic (metro) or 40% (non-metro)

Implemented in `hra_exemption()`, taking annual basic, annual HRA, rent paid and metro status.

## 4. Generating a certificate

![Form 16 list](../screenshots/form16-list.png)

Filter by financial year, status, branch or employee number. Generate individually or in bulk
across the workforce.

### Part A construction

`build_part_a_snapshot()`:

1. Resolve the **employment period** within the financial year — for a mid-year joiner or leaver
   this is a partial period, not April–March
2. Aggregate **TDS by quarter** — Q1 (Apr–Jun), Q2 (Jul–Sep), Q3 (Oct–Dec), Q4 (Jan–Mar)
3. Attach deposit and challan references
4. Snapshot employer and employee identity

### Part B construction

`build_part_b_snapshot()`:

```
Gross salary            = Σ payslip earnings across the FY
  − Exemptions          (HRA, LTA, others)
  − Standard deduction  (₹50,000 old regime · ₹75,000 new regime)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
= Income under the head Salaries
  ± Income / loss from house property   (capped at ₹2,00,000 loss)
  + Other income declared
─────────────────────────────────────────────
= Gross total income
  − Chapter VI-A deductions             (80C, 80D, 80CCD(1B), 80TTA, 80E … capped)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
= Total taxable income
  → Tax on slab rates                   (per elected regime)
  + Surcharge                           (if applicable)
  − Rebate under 87A                    (if within threshold)
  + Health & education cess @ 4%
─────────────────────────────────────────────
= Total tax liability
  − TDS already deducted
= Balance payable / refundable
```

### Why it is a *snapshot*

Part A and Part B are stored as snapshot records, not recomputed on each view. A published Form 16
is a legal document an employee may already have filed a return from. If it recalculated live, a
later salary correction would silently change a certificate already in the tax department's hands.
Snapshotting freezes the certificate; changing it requires an explicit revoke or rectification,
which leaves a trail.

## 5. Quarterly TDS and Q4 rounding absorption

TDS is deducted monthly but reported quarterly, and monthly figures are rounded. Twelve rounded
monthly deductions rarely sum to the rounded annual figure — a discrepancy of a few rupees.

The tax department reconciles the certificate against deposits, and a mismatch of any size flags.
The engine therefore **absorbs accumulated rounding difference into Q4**, so quarterly figures sum
exactly to the annual total while each quarter still reflects what was actually deducted.

---

## 6. Reconciliation

`reconciliation_diff()` compares:

- computed tax liability, against
- actual TDS deducted across the year, against
- TDS actually deposited via challans

Surfaced as a review step before publication. A mismatch here is precisely the condition that
generates a notice months later, so it is treated as blocking rather than informational.

---

## 7. Publish, revoke, rectify

```
DRAFT ──generate──► reviewed ──publish──► PUBLISHED ──► employee downloads
                                              │
                                              ├─ revoke ──► withdrawn from portal
                                              │
                                              └─ employee raises rectification
                                                        │
                                                        ▼
                                                 admin queue ──► corrected ──► reissued
```

![Rectifications](../screenshots/form16-rectifications.png)

**Revoke** pulls a certificate back if HR finds an error post-release. **Rectification** is the
employee-initiated path — they spot a wrong PAN or a missing exemption, raise a request against the
published certificate, and it enters the admin queue. Both paths are recorded, so the history of a
corrected certificate is auditable rather than lost in an email thread.

---

## 8. Regime handling

Certificates are generated under the employee's **elected regime**. The module implements:

- `compute_tax_old_regime()` — old slabs with full Chapter VI-A deductions
- `compute_tax_new_regime()` / `compute_tax_new_regime_2025()` — new slabs, higher standard
  deduction, minimal deductions
- `compute_surcharge()` — high-income surcharge on the computed tax

A scoped new-regime pilot flag exists in the service, allowing the newer computation to be rolled
out to a subset of employees before becoming the default — a controlled migration path rather than
a flag-day switch for a statutory calculation.

## Advantages

| Capability | Value |
|---|---|
| Generated from live payroll data | No re-keying between payroll and statutory filing |
| Snapshotted certificates | A filed certificate cannot silently change |
| Verified declarations only | Employees are not exposed to assessment-time shortfalls |
| Q4 rounding absorption | Quarterly figures reconcile exactly to annual |
| Built-in reconciliation | Mismatches caught before issue, not after a notice |
| Revoke + rectification workflow | Corrections are controlled and auditable |
| Bulk generation | An entire workforce's certificates in one operation |
| Statutory caps in one place | A Budget change is a constant edit |
